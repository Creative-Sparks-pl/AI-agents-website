# Subject lines and preheaders, v2 (Polish)

Written to the author's newsletter guide and past subjects (`scratch/CS - Newslettery - tematy.md`, `scratch/CS - Newslettery - tematy bilioteka.md`). The first round, written to the blog title rules, is in `subject-lines-v1.md`.

## What the guide changes against v1

- **Every subject should carry "Ja/My".** A subject anyone could have written goes in the bin. v1 mostly spoke about the skill, not about the author's own work.
- **Questions, digits, colons and a direct "you" are fine.** The past subjects use all of them ("3 sposoby…", "Myślisz, że przegrywasz ceną? …"), so the blog's bans on these do not apply here.
- **Periods stay.** The past subjects end with a period or a question mark, which settles the open question from v1.
- **Failure stories are the strongest format.** The only real one in this series is the audit of the agency's own site (audit email 2).
- **The preheader completes the subject.** It never repeats the subject, and it follows one of the guide's six formulas.
- **No exclamation marks, caps or "secret" clickbait.** Past subjects are mostly 30-70 characters and informal.

## Kept from the project rules

- Every claim comes from the email, `author-stories.md` or the author's published bio. A claim from outside the email body is flagged.
- A first version is never presented as a finished project.
- Where a subject describes full-version work, the subject or preheader says so, or the email's first sentence does.
- No em dashes, and no gendered forms for the reader ("zrobiłeś", "sam").
- Planner email 1 and planner email 3 should not both use the research-before-the-brief hook.

## Planner email 1 — `1-planowanie-dobry-wynik.md`

Pillar: getting a good plan from the free skill. Spark: the plan is only as good as the context and the conversation. The email has no story of the author's own, so "Ja/My" comes from `author-stories.md`.

1. **Recommended.** Transformation, in the first person · Sneak Peek
   - Subject: Pierwszy plan ze skilla traktuję jako punkt startowy, a nie gotowiec.
   - Preheader: I 3 inne sposoby, żeby był lepszy, zanim powstaną makiety.
   - The author's own words ("Traktuję je jako punkt startowy"), and they match the email's fourth tip. Loses: the email gives this as advice, not as the author's habit.
2. Numbers and specifics · Sneak Peek
   - Subject: 4 sposoby na lepszy plan ze Skilla do planowania stron.
   - Preheader: W tym: co zrobić z każdym z 3 poziomów pewności.
   - The clearest promise, and it names the reader's skill. Loses: no "Ja/My".
3. Diagnostic · Reveal
   - Subject: Akceptujesz plan ze skilla na „ok"? Zadaj mu najpierw jedno pytanie.
   - Preheader: Plan jest tak dobry, jak rozmowa, którą ze skillem prowadzisz.
   - Makes the reader check their own habit. Loses: the "one question" ("dlaczego") is only part of the email.
4. Problem-solution, in the first person · Reveal
   - Subject: Zanim zacznę plan, wrzucam do skilla brief i research o kliencie.
   - Preheader: Im więcej kontekstu dasz na starcie, tym mniej skill zgaduje.
   - A concrete habit from `author-stories.md` that matches the first tip. Loses: research is also a hook in email 3; use it in one email, not both.

## Audit email 1 — `1-audyt-dobry-wynik.md`

Pillar: reading the audit result well. Spark: everything depends on the page type the skill recognised, and low confidence means the AI is guessing. The email has no story of the author's own, so none of these has a real "Ja/My".

1. **Recommended.** Numbers and specifics · Sneak Peek
   - Subject: 3 rzeczy do sprawdzenia w wyniku audytu UX strony głównej.
   - Preheader: Pierwsza decyduje o całej reszcie wniosków.
   - Practical, and the preheader opens a gap. Loses: no "Ja/My".
2. Diagnostic, as a question · Reveal
   - Subject: Część wniosków z audytu to zgadywanie. Wiesz, które?
   - Preheader: Skill oznacza je wprost. Pokazuję, gdzie patrzeć, zanim cokolwiek zmienisz.
   - The strongest curiosity, and honesty builds trust. Loses: day 1 opens on a limit of the product.
3. Challenger · Reassurance
   - Subject: Twoja wiedza o klientach jest ważniejsza niż wzorce, które zna AI.
   - Preheader: Audyt daje diagnozę. Decyzję o priorytetach podejmujesz Ty.
   - Pushes back on AI hype, so it stands out in the inbox. Loses: says little about what is inside.
4. Curiosity · Sneak Peek
   - Subject: Audyt trwa 20-30 minut. Najważniejsze zaczyna się, gdy dostaniesz wynik.
   - Preheader: Jak czytać wnioski, żeby nie zmieniać strony na podstawie zgadywania.
   - Sets an honest time expectation. Loses: the longest of the four.
