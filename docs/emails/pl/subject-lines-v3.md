# Subject lines and preheaders, v3 (Polish)

Written to the BrandIGNITE newsletter guide (`g:\Mój dysk\Temp\AI poradniki\BrandIGNITE - przewodnik pisania newsletterów.md`, section 3, "Wytyczne do tytułów"). The subject line is the guide's title, the preheader its subtitle. Earlier rounds: `subject-lines-v1.md` (blog title rules), `subject-lines-v2.md` (Creative Sparks newsletter guide).

## How the guide is applied

- **Three approaches, one per variant:** Curiosity & Tension, Value & Specificity, Authority & Insight. Each email gets four variants, and the fourth goes to whichever approach fits that email best.
- **The subject asks a question or makes a strong claim. The preheader develops it and adds context.** The guide's subtitle moves are used: "Podpowiedź: …", "Nie chodzi o …", a number, a reason.
- **Authority comes from real numbers, not research.** These emails cite no studies. So "authority" means the product's own figures (16 page types, 13 frameworks, 50+ patterns) and the author's own times (2-3 hours, at most 10-20h, about 2 hours), plus insights the email states outright.
- **Short and confident.** The guide's examples run 30-60 characters.
- **Not used:** the guide's sources list and research-based structure, which suit articles, not these emails.

## Kept from the project rules

- Every claim comes from the email body or `author-stories.md`. A claim from outside the body is flagged.
- A first version is never presented as a finished project.
- No em dashes, no gendered forms for the reader, and no hedge words such as "naprawdę" (the guide's own example uses it; the anti-slop filter bans it).
- Within a series, no two subjects share the same hook, and "Podpowiedź:" appears at most once.

## Planner email 1 — `1-planowanie-dobry-wynik.md`

The one big thing: the plan is only as good as what you give the skill and how you talk to it.

1. **Recommended.** Curiosity & Tension
   - Subject: Ten sam skill, dwa różne plany. Od czego to zależy?
   - Preheader: Od tego, co mu dasz na starcie i jak z nim rozmawiasz.
   - Opens a gap the reader wants closed on day 1: "how do I get the good plan?". Loses: "dwa różne plany" is a consequence of the email's claim, not a line in it.
2. Curiosity & Tension
   - Subject: Gdzie skill do planowania stron zgaduje?
   - Preheader: Mówi to wprost. Każde założenie ma poziom pewności: wysoki, średni albo niski.
   - Short, and honest about AI. Loses: covers the second tip only.
3. Value & Specificity
   - Subject: Jak wyciągnąć lepszy plan strony z darmowego skilla.
   - Preheader: 4 nawyki, które warto mieć od pierwszej rozmowy ze skillem.
   - The clearest promise. Loses: the least tension.
4. Authority & Insight
   - Subject: Plan z AI to hipoteza, a nie decyzja.
   - Preheader: Skill zna wzorce, ale nie zna Twojego projektu. Dlatego plan jest punktem wyjścia.
   - A strong thesis in the guide's tone. Loses: "hipoteza" comes from the site's process description, not from this email.

   - Preheader: Teraz mówię skillowi, co zmienić. Pierwsza wersja makiet zajmuje mi 2-3 godziny.
