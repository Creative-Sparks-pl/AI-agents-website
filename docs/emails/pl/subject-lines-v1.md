# Subject lines and preview text — Polish working draft

Procedure: `Tytuly.md` from MKT-Blog, without its "must contain B2B" rule. The subject line stands in for the H1 and the preview text for the lead. Accepted pairs go to a new library, `subject-lines.md`, at the root of this folder; rejected directions go to the project's `memory.md`.

How the procedure is applied to email (writer's call, not in the rules):
- Subject up to about 75 characters, key words first, because mobile inboxes cut at about 40. Preview up to about 90.
- The collision check compares the subject, the preview, the email's first paragraph and its first heading. A colliding variant is still allowed, but it lists the body change it would need. The bodies are approved, so none of the recommended pairs collide.
- Every email has its own file. Emails 3 and 4 have the same body in both series apart from the opening, so for now each shares one pair across both series; emails 1 and 2 get one pair per series. The twin check (no two subjects with the same construction and hook) runs within a series.
- The final period stays, per the procedure's fixed rule.
