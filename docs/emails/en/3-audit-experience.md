**Subject:** I stopped fixing wireframes by hand in Figma. Now I say what to change.

**Preheader:** This is how I design changes in the full UX Design Skill.

---

The free Homepage UX Audit Skill comes from the full [UX Design Skill](https://agents.creativesparks.pl/en/ux-design-skill/), which I use every day, on agency projects and my own. I can't imagine going back to the way I used to work.

A first version of the plan and wireframes takes me 2-3 hours. That's my starting point for discussion and iteration. Getting all the way to the final wireframe now takes 10-20 hours at most.


## No more clicking around in Figma. I just say what to change.

In Figma, or any other tool, everything had to be done by hand, and it took a lot longer. Now I make a single fix here and there, or collect feedback and apply it all in one go. I describe in my own words what to change or what's bugging me, and the skill and I work out the final version together.


## Final copy is no longer the bottleneck.

The content clients send us is often mediocre: long descriptions in Word, page after page. The skill pulls out what matters to the reader and turns it into website copy. That saves me a ton of work.


## I research the client before the brief.

A lot of what I need to know about a client is already online, so I don't have to ask them. I use Google Deep Research or Perplexity to get a handle on the category, the business model, and the typical services, then hand that document to the skill. It saves me a lot of work, interviews, and calls.

It also gets me to the brief faster. The skill only asks about the specifics instead of starting from zero.


## Try this on your next project.

Start by pulling together what you already have: the brief, materials, research on the company. The more context you give up front, the less the AI has to guess, and the more time you have for the decisions it can't make for you.
