**Preheader:** Mostly in how we talked about what makes us different, and to whom.

## I start client redesigns by wireframing their current site.

It gives me a fast starting point for the client conversation. From there, I put together the redesign plan, so I can scope and price the project properly from day one.


## When we reworked our strategy, the audit exposed gaps in our own messaging.

We were repositioning and expanding our services, so the site was getting rebuilt anyway. I caught quite a few mistakes in how we described what makes us different, who we were talking to, and how we said it. The homepage was a bit cluttered, and the services page was light on specifics.


## I turned the findings into a redesign plan and wireframes.

I started with the messaging foundations, since that's where the audit found the gaps. Only then did I plan the new structure and the wireframes with final copy. I did it all with the same skill.


## Where to start?

Run the audit on your own homepage, or on a client's before your first meeting. You'll have concrete findings to start the conversation about changes.
