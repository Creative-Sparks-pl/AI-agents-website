# English subject lines and preheaders — options

Written with `subject-lines-procedure.md`. Option 1 in every email is a native English version of the Polish pair the author picked; options 2-4 are alternatives from other approaches. Mix any subject with any preheader.

   - Preheader: The plan is only as good as your conversation with the skill.

   - Subject: Same skill, two different plans. What makes the difference?
   - Preheader: What you give it at the start, and how you talk to it.
