**Subject:** 3 things to check in your homepage UX audit.

**Preheader:** The first one shapes every other finding.

---

All the free Homepage UX Audit Skill needs is your homepage URL. The audit can take 20-30 minutes, and you end up with an HTML file: a wireframe of your page with findings pinned to specific elements. How much you get out of it depends on how you read it.


## First, check the site type.

Every finding compares your site to the standard for its type, so everything else hinges on this. The skill tells you how it identified the type and how sure it is.


## Check how confident each finding is.

Each finding is tagged high, medium, or low confidence. Low means the AI is guessing, so verify those before you change anything.


## Push back on findings that don't make sense to you.

You know your customers and your market better than any pattern the skill knows. Ask why, or ask for alternatives. The audit gives you a diagnosis, but what to fix first is still your call.
