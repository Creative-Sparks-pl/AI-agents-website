**Subject:** 4 ways to get a better plan from the Website Planning Skill.

**Preheader:** The plan is only as good as your conversation with the skill.

---

The free Website Planning Skill asks about your goals, your audience, and your context, then turns your answers into a brief and a sitemap with content guidelines. How good that plan is comes down to two things: what you feed it, and how you talk to it.


## Start with more context.

You can build the brief together in conversation: the skill interviews you and drafts it from your answers. Or you can come prepared with a brief, company materials, and anything else you already have. The more context it gets up front, the less it has to guess.


## Check the reasoning and the confidence levels.

Every decision comes with its reasoning and the options it ruled out. Every assumption gets a confidence tag: high, medium, or low. Keep the highs, double-check the mediums, and fill in the gaps behind the lows before you move on.


## Don't settle for the first answer.

Ask why, ask for alternatives, tell it to cut things down. If none of the options work, say what's off and it'll suggest new ones.


## Treat the plan as a starting point.

The skill knows the patterns, but it doesn't have your experience or the full picture. Use the plan to kick off the discussion. The wireframes and the design come after.
