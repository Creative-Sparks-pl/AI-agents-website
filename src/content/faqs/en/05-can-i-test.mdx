q: Can I test it before buying?
---

Yes. You can choose between two free skills taken from the full version. If you already have a site, the Homepage UX Audit Skill analyzes your homepage and prepares a wireframe with findings pinned to specific elements of the page. If you're planning a site, the Website Planning Skill asks about your project, plans the sitemap and lays out the sections of every page. No account, just your email. Decide to buy after a real test on your own project.
