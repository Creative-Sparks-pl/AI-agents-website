q: What exactly am I buying? Is it a subscription?
---

A one-time license. Pay once, own forever. 12 months of free updates are included. When the update window closes, your installed copy keeps working with no time limit. You can extend updates for another year at about half the package price, or stay on the version you have. No accounts and no logins. At start-up the skill only checks its license, and it stops only after a refund or when the link has been blocked, for example after it was shared with other people.
