q: Do I need to know UX to use this?
---

No. Sixteen domain knowledge packs, thirteen frameworks, and 50+ section patterns are built in. The skill loads the right set once it detects the page type. Your role is the strategic decisions (who you serve, what the goal is) and the final pick at every checkpoint. The technical UX side is done for you. That's why a generalist designer can take a project in a domain they don't know cold.

That said, UX knowledge makes a real difference. AI models are good at exploring lots of options, but they don't always pick the best one. That's where your experience comes in.
