q: What happens to my project data?
---

The skill runs in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, on your own account. The conversation falls under that tool's privacy policy and settings; the skill only gives it instructions for running the process. Your project files stay with you. At start-up the skill checks its license and sends us only your customer ID, the package name and the version number, nothing from the conversation. When you audit an existing site, its address goes to our page-reading service.
