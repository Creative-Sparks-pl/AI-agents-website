title: Faster work, fewer questions
description: |
  The agent works faster, asks less and does not make up data it does not know.
changes:
  - "<strong>Faster, with fewer unnecessary questions.</strong> In our tests, sessions finish about a quarter faster on average, and their results meet our quality criteria more often. The agent infers your differentiator from what it already knows and only asks you to confirm it, starts from your brief if you paste one, and in an audit does not ask about scope when your request already makes it clear."
  - "<strong>No made-up data in designed wireframes.</strong> A phone number, email, address or opening hours the agent does not know appear as fields to fill in, in square brackets, for example [phone]. Claims not backed by project data (certifications, numbers, guarantees) stay in as a proposal for you to decide on, marked with a low-confidence note on that element."
  - "<strong>The right language and labels.</strong> The agent replies in the language you write to it in, even if your system is set to another one, and the buttons and labels in the project plan, wireframes and audit are in the project's language. An English project plan no longer shows names in curly braces, such as {Confirmed}."
