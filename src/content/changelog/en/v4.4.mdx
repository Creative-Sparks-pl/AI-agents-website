title: Mobile view for wireframes and documents
description: |
  You can now view wireframes, audits, the project plan and the messaging canvas comfortably on a phone or in a narrow window, with the same content laid out for a narrow screen. Installing and updating the assistant is now one step.
changes:
  - "<strong>Install and update in one step.</strong> Point your coding assistant (Claude Code, Codex or Cursor) at INSTALL.md in the download and it installs the assistant or updates the version you already have. It shows a plan first and waits for your yes, keeps your edits to files (asking about each edited file), backs up what it replaces, and removes files the new version no longer has."
  - "<strong>A \"Mobile view\" checkbox.</strong> In the bottom bar of wireframes and audits, right after the page picker, you will find a \"Mobile view\" checkbox. Tick it and the page narrows to 390px, the most common phone width, and your choice is remembered for each file."
  - "<strong>Every wireframe block fits a narrow screen.</strong> On a narrower page, rows of three or four columns become two, and at phone width everything stacks into a single column with smaller headings, in a narrow browser window and with the side panel open too. The nav bar then shows the logo, the call to action as a compact button, and a menu icon that opens the list of links."
  - "<strong>The project plan and messaging canvas on your phone.</strong> The project plan, the messaging canvas and the copy draft fit the screen you open them on: two columns on a narrower screen, one on a phone. You can read them comfortably on a phone, without scrolling sideways."
  - "<strong>Comfortable review in a narrow window.</strong> The download buttons fold into a single \"Download ▾\" menu, and in a narrow window the side panel opens over the page, starts closed, and you pick pages from a list. In wireframes and audits the view checkboxes fold into a \"View ▾\" menu, so the bottom bar fits even on a phone."
  - "<strong>Downloading the audit as HTML works again.</strong> The audit's HTML download saves the audit again, and downloaded audit files are easy to tell apart from wireframes by their name."
