title: Copy draft and a stronger messaging canvas
description: |
  Messaging Foundations now ends with a draft of your site copy, the messaging canvas shows more clearly what is proven, and talking to the agent is simpler.
changes:
  - "<strong>A draft of your site copy.</strong> Once the messaging canvas is built, the agent can prepare a copy draft: hero, social proof bar, value propositions, \"How it works\", \"Why us\", FAQ and calls to action. Wireframes take their text from this draft, so you do not start from empty fields."
  - "<strong>Pillars checked on three dimensions.</strong> For each messaging pillar you see separately whether the product actually does it, whether customers want it, and whether it works in practice, each rated Confirmed, Use with caution or No evidence. When several pillars lack evidence, the agent offers a research brief or a pilot plan."
  - "<strong>A messaging canvas that shows more.</strong> A new capabilities cell marks each capability as a Differentiator, a Table Stake or a Delighter, and the outcomes customers expect are ordered by weight, from Must-have, through Deciding factor, to Pleasant surprise. Unfilled cells are faded, so you see progress at a glance."
  - "<strong>Several service lines on one canvas.</strong> If you offer several service lines under one brand, the canvas has a switch between the whole-brand view and a view for each line, with its own target audience, alternatives and outcomes. With a single product, the canvas looks the same as before."
  - "<strong>The agent challenges your assumptions.</strong> When you put forward an important claim, the agent offers counter-arguments on its own, says where you are right, and proposes a synthesis or a third way. You do not have to ask."
  - "<strong>Simpler conversation.</strong> The agent speaks plain language, without jargon or internal labels, asks before a longer set of questions whether you want them one at a time or all at once, and presents decisions briefly: question, options, recommendation. After saving a file, it does not copy the content into the chat; it points to the file and the key finding."
