title: Site audit, clickable deliverables, audit-to-redesign path
description: |
  Major update. A new site-audit workflow, automatic site fetching, clickable deliverables in the browser, and an audit-to-redesign path.
changes:
  - "<strong>Mapping existing sites into wireframes, now more accurate and automatic.</strong> The agent reads your site layout straight from the browser instead of guessing from screenshots, so the wireframe reflects the real structure: hero, sections, columns, cards, navigation. It also works on heavily JavaScript-driven sites that used to lose most of their content."
  - "<strong>Web (Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini), no copy-paste.</strong> The agent fetches the site by URL on its own and builds a mobile wireframe (around 400px wide). It does not interrupt you to paste content. The full desktop version is available in the IDE editions (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity)."
  - "<strong>New workflow: Site audit.</strong> Drop in a URL. The agent compares your site against knowledge of 17 page types (B2B Industrial, SaaS, ecommerce, portfolio, NGO, data-driven content, and more) and produces a single HTML file with two deliverables: an architecture analysis plus annotations on specific elements (where a call to action is missing, whether the conversion path is consistent, whether the hero communicates the value proposition). No brief, no analytics, no surveys required."
  - "<strong>You decide what to audit.</strong> Homepage only (the recommended, fastest option), the full site (complete coverage), or your own list of subpages. The agent proposes a candidate list based on the sitemap and domain knowledge. You accept, edit, or skip."
  - "<strong>Audit findings feed the redesign.</strong> If you want to rebuild the site after the audit, your findings (accepted / rejected / under discussion) flow automatically into the next steps: information plan, page archetype selection, anatomy of each template. You do not have to retype anything."
  - "<strong>Deliverables in the browser with clickable UI.</strong> Open the audit or wireframe in your browser. Every finding is clickable (the detail opens in the left panel), has three buttons (accept / reject / mark for discussion), and at the bottom you get an export-to-Markdown button for the decision brief. Findings are anchored to specific elements (for example a hero, a button, a particular section)."
  - "<strong>Free Audit Demo, a lightweight variant of the skill for portfolios and digital products.</strong> Fetches a URL, classifies the site, produces a short audit of the homepage. No project setup, no context. Ideal for demos or quick feedback."
  - '<strong>Better copy quality in Messaging Foundations.</strong> Target-audience naming (ICP) is picked from 6 methodologies: by role, industry, life situation, or trigger. Main headlines are drawn from 8 proven patterns. The "Why us" section comes in three approaches: comparison with competitors, complement to existing tools, or alternative to avoiding the problem. Pillars with evidence in 3 tiers: Hard (metrics, case studies) / Soft (demos, capability) / Hypothesis (logic, early signals).'
