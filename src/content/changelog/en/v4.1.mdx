title: More faithful wireframes and in-chat feedback
description: |
  Wireframes and audits of existing sites stay closer to the original, and you can send feedback about the agent without leaving the conversation.
changes:
  - "<strong>Wireframes of existing sites closer to the original.</strong> Headings carry the same weight in the wireframe as on the site, animations and illustrations loaded by scripts show up as image placeholders, and the breadcrumb trail and announcement strip sit where they are on the site. Before saving, the agent checks that the wireframe has not lost any content, so client logos and numbers no longer disappear or repeat."
  - "<strong>Audit findings in the right place.</strong> Finding markers land on the right element and the right page; before, they could go missing or end up on the homepage. The agent checks the file before handing it over, so an audit never opens blank, and large audits save without errors on Windows too."
  - "<strong>Send feedback straight from the conversation (IDE editions).</strong> Say you want to report a bug, suggest an idea, share feedback or ask a question, and the agent helps you describe it, shows you a preview to approve and posts it to the public feedback forum on GitHub. You give an email for replies only if you want to."
